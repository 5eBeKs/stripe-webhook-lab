Stripe webhook lab: a ticket app in two versions, on a stand with no way out
network: example.com does not resolve, 1.1.1.1 is unreachable, api.stripe.com is the stand's Stripe

R1  A paid checkout gives the buyer one paid ticket with a code, whichever comes first, the webhook
    or the success page. A checkout left unpaid gives none.
R2  A ticket is given only for money received, as Stripe reports it: not for an event without
    Stripe's signature, not while a bank debit is on its way, never for one that failed. No second
    checkout while it is on its way.
R3  A settled ticket stays settled: an event delivered again or the success page opened again does
    not undo a refund. The organiser's figures show every refund.
R4  A refund reaches the ticket even when it comes before the ticket was confirmed.
R5  The webhook answers 2xx only once the change is saved, so that Stripe delivers the event again
    otherwise.
R6  Only a payment makes a paid ticket: a buyer cannot write one through the database API.
R7  The door admits a paid or partly refunded ticket, once.
